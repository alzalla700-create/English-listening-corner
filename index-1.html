A Day in Egypt

PARAGRAPH 02 American English

Ras Mohamed National Park

Ras Mohamed is a special place because it has beautiful nature. It is known for its clear blue sea, colorful fish, and coral reefs. Many people visit Ras Mohamed to swim, snorkel, and dive. The park is home to turtles, dolphins, birds, and many plants. There are also mountains and trees near the beach. It is a protected area, so people must keep it clean and safe.

PARAGRAPH 03 American English

Life in the Village

Life in the village is simple but busy. Many people work hard to support their families. Some go fishing early in the morning. Others work in fish factories or fix boats. In some places, people grow fruits and vegetables. They water the plants, harvest the crops, and feed the animals. On Fridays, they go to the market to sell food. In desert areas, people make carpets and rugs. They spin and dye wool using natural colors. Every day, people help each other and enjoy the quiet life in the village.

PARAGRAPH 04 American English

A Day at Your School

My school day starts early. I wake up around 6:30 a.m., get dressed, and eat a quick breakfast. Then I go to school. The school day begins at 7:30 a.m. We stand in line, salute the flag, and sing the national anthem. After that, we go to our classrooms and start our lessons. The first subject is usually Arabic. My favorite subject is P.E. The school day ends at 1:30 p.m. I feel a bit tired, but I'm happy. I go home, eat lunch, rest a little, and then do my homework.
